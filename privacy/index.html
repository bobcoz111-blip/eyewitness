Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Eyewitness is a free weekly game. We keep what we collect small and we never sell it.

Your email address

If you sign up for the weekly case, we keep your email address so we can send you each new case. Our email service is MailerLite, which stores the list for us. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time.

Your game answers

Your answers and score stay in your own browser. We don't send them to us or store them.

Amazon links

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Some links on this site, like the games in the Game Room, go to Amazon. If you buy something after clicking, Amazon may pay us a small fee at no extra cost to you. Amazon may use cookies to track that click. See Amazon's own privacy notice for how they use that data.

Basic site data

Like most websites, our hosting provider and fonts service (Google Fonts) may log basic technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open, to keep the site running and secure. We may also count visits to see which cases people enjoy.

Children

The game is family-friendly, but the email sign-up is meant for adults. If you are under 13, please ask a parent to sign up for you.

Questions

Just reply to any Eyewitness email and we'll answer, or remove your email from our list on request.
